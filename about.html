About Us

Your Trusted Travel Partner in Jharkhand

Welcome to LavKush Taxi Service

LavKush Taxi Service has been serving travelers in Jharkhand with dedication and reliability. Founded and operated by Sonu Kumar, we specialize in providing comfortable and safe transportation for both local and tourist travelers.

Our mission is to make your journey memorable by offering exceptional service, local expertise, and competitive rates. Whether you're visiting the majestic waterfalls of Ranchi, the scenic beauty of Netarhat, or the wildlife of Betla, we ensure your travel experience is smooth and enjoyable.

Our Story

What started as a single vehicle service has grown into a trusted name in Jharkhand's tourism sector. With years of experience navigating the beautiful landscapes of Ranchi, Netarhat, and Betla, we understand what travelers need most - reliability, safety, and local knowledge.

Our fleet of well-maintained vehicles and team of experienced drivers ensure that every journey with us is comfortable and secure. We take pride in being more than just a taxi service; we're your local guide and travel companion.
